Every reduction, anchored to its real prior price.

PriceAnchor is a Shopify app for EU Omnibus compliance. It records your price history per variant and per market, then renders the statutory lowest price of the last 30 days next to every sale — with the “% off” computed from that prior price, never from a stale compare-at anchor.

Setup in three steps

  1. Install PriceAnchor. Price history starts recording automatically from product webhooks, plus a daily snapshot backup.
  2. Let history accumulate. The 30-day prior price needs observations; the Products page shows what has been captured so far.
  3. Add the “Lowest price” block to your product template. Shoppers see the statutory line next to every reduction, per market.

Honest constraints

Does PriceAnchor convert currency?
No. Each market keeps its own price history in its own currency. A German price drop never sets the French reference price.
Is the % off taken from compare-at?
No. Compare-at is a free-text merchant field with no link to the Art 6a prior price. The % off is computed from the 30-day prior price (Aldi rule); the stale-sale guard flags old compare-at anchors.
Which themes work?
All Shopify OS 2.0 themes. Vintage themes are not supported.
What data leaves my store?
Almost none: store domain, plan state, and price snapshots needed for the history. Lowest prices live in your products' metafields. No customer data, ever.
