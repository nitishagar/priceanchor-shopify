Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

PriceAnchor (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the PriceAnchor Shopify application. This policy explains what data we collect from merchants, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Store information: store domain, plan tier, display preferences.
  • Product data: product/variant identifiers and price snapshots, read only to build per-market price history.
  • Lowest-price metafields: computed prior prices are stored as app-owned metafields on your own products.

We do not collect any customer data. Storefront rendering reads your own product metafields in the shopper's browser.

2. How we use your data

  • Recording price history and computing per-market prior prices.
  • Writing lowest-price metafields to your products and clearing stale compare-at anchors (when enabled).
  • Enforcing plan limits and showing plan status.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored encrypted on Cloudflare Workers and Neon PostgreSQL, transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Shopify session tokens use Shopify's official session storage and authenticate API calls on your behalf only.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Neon) process data only as necessary to operate the service.

5. Retention

  • While installed: settings and plan state for the subscription duration.
  • After uninstall: automatic deletion once Shopify sends the data-erasure request. App-owned lowest-price metafields stay on your products until you remove them.

6. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, and portability under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws. Uninstalling triggers automatic deletion.

7. Cookies and tracking

No cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers. Admin auth uses Shopify session tokens.

8. Changes

Updates appear here with a new “Last updated” date.
