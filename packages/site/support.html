Getting started

After installing PriceAnchor, let history accumulate and add the storefront block:

  1. Wait for the first price snapshots — history is captured automatically from product webhooks plus a daily snapshot.
  2. Go to Online Store > Themes > Customize, open the product template.
  3. Click Add block > Apps > Lowest price and click Save.

Frequently asked questions

Which prior price does PriceAnchor show?
The EU Omnibus prior price: the lowest price of the last 30 days (Art 6a), tracked per market and currency.
Do price changes update automatically?
Yes. Every change is captured from product webhooks, with a daily snapshot as backup.
What is the stale-sale guard?
Shopify never expires compare-at prices. The guard flags anchors older than your thresholds and can auto-clear them.
Which themes are supported?
All Shopify OS 2.0 themes. Vintage themes are not supported.

Troubleshooting

Lowest price is not appearing
Check that the app block is added to the product template, the product has history on the Products page, and you are on an OS 2.0 theme. Then hard-refresh.
Stale sale label won't go away
Check your stale-sale guard thresholds in Settings, enable auto-clear, or clear the compare-at price on the variant in Shopify admin.
